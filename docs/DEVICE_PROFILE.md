# Device profile — CMF Watch Pro 2

## Owner-confirmed

- Product shown on watch About screen: CMF Watch Pro 2.
- Watch model shown on watch: D398.
- Firmware version shown by companion app: 1.0.0.73 (up to date at screenshot time).
- Companion app's device string: CMF Watch Pro 2-DB35.

## Interpretation and unknowns

D398 is the published product model for CMF Watch Pro 2. DB35 is recorded as a separate app-reported device string; it is not assumed to be a hardware revision or firmware board name.

The following remain community-reported and must be verified against this exact unit:
- Actions ATS3089C platform.
- Zephyr RTOS 2.7 and LVGL.
- 466 × 466 round AMOLED display.
- Exact board name, panel/touch drivers, pinmux, sensors, flash map, bootloader, signatures, and recovery.

See [FIRMWARE_BASELINE.md](FIRMWARE_BASELINE.md) and [INSTALLATION_READINESS.md](INSTALLATION_READINESS.md).

Serial number and Bluetooth address from the user's screenshots are intentionally not stored in the repository.
